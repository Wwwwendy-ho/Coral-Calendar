# Alissa's Move & Learn Plan

A daily tutoring planner built for Alissa. She's 12, autistic, and working at about a first-grade level. Every day runs the same four lessons in the same order, with movement built into each one:

**Flashcards → Reading comprehension → Addition → ASL**

The heart of the app is the **Tide Chart**, the day's schedule. It tells the tutor what to do, what to print, and what to set up. It also keeps a record of how each lesson went.

---

## A day at a glance

A full day is about 2½ hours in nine blocks. There are no clock times, because sessions don't always start at the same time.

| High Tide (regular day) | Minutes | Low Tide (gentler day) | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check-in and warm-up | 5 | Calm start | 10 |
| Flashcards | 25 | Flashcards | 15 |
| Active break | 10 | Calm break | 15 |
| Reading comprehension | 40 | Reading comprehension | 25 |
| Snack and outdoor recess | 15 | Snack and rest | 20 |
| Addition | 25 | Addition | 20 |
| Active break | 10 | Calm break | 15 |
| ASL | 15 | ASL | 15 |
| Cool-down | 5 | Quiet finish | 15 |

**High Tide** is the regular day, with a movement game in every lesson. **Low Tide** is for hard days: the same order, fewer demands, and no choices for her to make. You can switch the whole day, or just one block, between the two.

---

## How it's designed around Alissa

- **The same order every day.** Only the games change, so the day stays predictable.
- **Visual structure.** The day strip, progress dots and "Then: …" preview show what's coming next.
- **Transition warnings.** The timer warns 1, 2 or 5 minutes before the end, and ends with a gentle wave sound instead of an alarm.
- **Clear choices.** When she gets to choose, the options are listed as buttons. Low Tide takes choices away entirely.
- **An alternative for every block.** If she doesn't want to do something, each block has a movement option and a quiet-space option.
- **Calming movement.** Heavy-work moves like wall push-ups and bear crawls, low music, and countdowns instead of sudden stops.
- **No competition.** She only ever tries to beat her own score.
- **Treated as a 12-year-old.** The work is at her level, but the activities aren't babyish.

## Features

### The Tide Chart (daily schedule)
- **Choosing a day:** weekday buttons for quick picks, plus a **month calendar** for any day.
- **Rescheduling:** **drag a lesson** to another day, by mouse or by press-and-hold on a touchscreen. If the day already has a lesson, choose **Shift the rest over** (that lesson and every later one move one school day) or **Swap**. If a move would put lessons out of order, the app asks first.
- **Shift the rest over:** a button by the day's title that moves that day's lesson to the next school day and every later lesson over by one, keeping the order.
- **Schedule changes:** every change is listed under the month calendar with its own **Undo**, plus one button to put every lesson back on its planned day.
- **Day at a glance:** a strip shows every block sized by its minutes. Finished blocks fill in, and tapping a block jumps to it.
- **Get ready list:** everything to print and set out for that day's games.
- **Explanations:** tap any activity to see why it's there, what you need, how to play, and easier or harder versions.
- **Game library:** every game grouped by lesson. **Switch game** swaps one in for a single day or for every week.
- **Timer:** a big countdown on screen that shrinks out of the way when you scroll or tap. Timer settings cover the sound, the volume and the warning time.
- **Notes about this day:** a free-text box saved with each lesson.
- **Edit plan:** change any block, part, time, choice or alternative.

### Logging as she works
Tap a colour, then tap each item as she does it:

| | Green | Blue | Orange | Pink |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Words | Read confidently | Read quietly | Read incorrectly | Couldn't read |
| Addition facts | Correct | Needed help | Incorrect | — |
| ASL signs | On her own | With help | — | Not yet |

**Worksheet highlighting:** open a worksheet and tap words right on the page to colour them. The printed PDF includes the highlights and a colour key.

### Week and month summary
This explains the direction of each day in the context of the week or month: what's new, what's review, how addition and ASL move along, and which blocks are on Low Tide. It also totals what you've logged and lists words to revisit. In the Claude version, **Write the summary with Claude** reads your notes and logs, then writes a fuller explanation and suggests changes.

### Her view
A full-screen view for Alissa, showing one task at a time:
- Progress dots, a big sea-creature picture, the task's steps, and "Then: …"
- Big buttons that press like real buttons, with a ripple, a tiny buzz on phones, and soft sounds (with a mute button)
- **I'm done** plays a chime, makes the button and picture wiggle, and bursts out bubbles and stars before the next task slides in
- A ring timer that empties as time runs down
- **Hold to exit** (about 1½ seconds), so she can't leave by accident

### Printing
Every PDF opens as a **preview first**. Nothing prints or downloads until you choose to.
- **Print schedule:** the day's plan, with a checklist and the get-ready list.
- **Print everything for the day:** the schedule, that day's worksheets, and every card set the games use, in one PDF.
- **Card sets:** word cards, the week's cards, addition fact cards, sign cards and answer letters.
- **Worksheets:** single worksheets, or a whole folder at once.

Cards fill the page edge to edge, so there's no border to trim. Stack the pages in order, cut down the columns, put the left stack on top of the next, then cut across the rows from the top. The cards come out in order, and each is numbered so you can check.

## The curriculum

### Reading: the worksheet bank
Three folders with 20 sheets each:

| Folder | Level | Used on | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Word Family** | Phonics | Every day | 5 family words, a short story read 3 times, "What will they do first?", a drawing |
| **At the Place** | Level 1 | School days 1–10 of the month | 3 short sentences, 3 questions (a–c) |
| **Reading Comprehension** | Level 3 | School days 11–20 | A longer story, 4 questions (a–d) |

- **New sheets each month.** September uses sheets 1–10 and October uses 11–20, so sheets don't repeat until a folder has been used up.
- **Planned review days.** School days 21 and up are review days. They reuse the sheet with the most orange or pink marks from earlier that month.
- **Tags.** Each day's sheet is labelled New, Repeat or Review.
- **Word cards.** Each day's cards come from that day's word family, Phonics Words, the story's vocabulary, and a few sight words.

### Addition
There are 65 facts where the first number is 0–9 and the answer is 10 or less. They're taught in waves by school day of the month:

| School days | Facts |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | 0, 1, 2 + ___ |
| 6–10 | 3, 4, 5 + ___ |
| 11–15 | 6, 7, 8, 9 + ___ |
| 16 and up | All 65 mixed, with extra practice on facts that make 10 |

The matching Coral Cove flashcard decks only include answers of 10 or less.

### ASL
- **99 signs**, taken from Alissa's ASL vocabulary list, in sets of nine.
- **A balanced nine-day cycle for each set.** She practises five signs a day, and every sign is practised exactly 5 times. No sign goes more than one day without practice. Each new sign is practised again the next day.
- **A picture for each sign:** its drawing, a 3-second moving picture, a description, and a link to a real video.

The moving pictures show where the hand moves, but not changes in handshape. Always check a sign against its video before teaching it.

## Files

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole app (page, styles and code) |
| `ws/` | The 60 worksheets: `wf-1…20` (Word Family), `l1-1…20` (At the Place), `l3-1…20` (Reading Comprehension), each as a PDF and a preview picture |
| `wsboxes.json` | Where each word sits on each worksheet, used for tap-to-highlight |
| `asl-media.js` | The 3-second moving pictures for all 99 signs, bundled into one file |
| `pdfjs/` | Helper file that shows PDF previews |

These libraries load from cdnjs: **jsPDF** (making PDFs), **pdf-lib** (combining PDFs) and **PDF.js** (previews). The fonts are **Fredoka** and **Nunito Sans** from Google Fonts.

## Good to know

- **Holidays:** the calendar treats every weekday as a school day. Drag lessons around holidays, or leave the day empty.
- **Repeating sheets:** the 20 Word Family sheets get used every month, and the comprehension sheets start repeating in November. Adding more sheets pushes those repeats further out.
- **Sounds:** they only play after the first tap, because browsers require it, and they follow the device's volume and mute switch.
- **Privacy:** the worksheets include Alissa's name. Keep the site private if that matters.
